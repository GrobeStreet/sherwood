Public data belongs to the public

A whole industry charges for records the government already gives away. Sherwood is the free side of that forest.

How it works

When you decode a VIN or a hull number here, Sherwood reads the answer live from the official free source — NHTSA for vehicles, the U.S. Coast Guard standard for boats — and shows it to you in plain language. There's no account, no paywall, and no "report" to buy. Vehicle lookups query NHTSA directly from your browser; boat hull numbers are decoded entirely on your device. Either way, nothing is stored and nothing is sent anywhere but the government source you're asking about.

What we won't do

We won't pretend a free decode is a full history. Some records genuinely aren't free — a car's title brands and odometer history live in the federal NMVTIS database (a few dollars through an approved provider); accident history is Carfax/AutoCheck; a boat's title and liens live with your state agency. When that's the case, we say so plainly and point you to the real source — we don't wrap a paid link in a "free report" and call it ours. No fake urgency, no upsells, no overclaiming.

Our sources
