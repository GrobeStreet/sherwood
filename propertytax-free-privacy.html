PropertyTax Free — Privacy Policy

Draft for pre-release verification • November 27, 2026. This policy describes the current planned app architecture. It must be reconciled with the final shipped binary and App Store privacy disclosures before launch.

Personal records remain on the phone

Saved property cases, homeowner statements, imported photos and exported documents are stored in the iPhone app's local storage. We do not operate a user-account, analytics, advertising or proprietary property-lookup backend for this app. The app does not automatically upload homeowner evidence to Sherwood.

Official data requests

When you search a street address or parcel identification number (PIN), the search goes from your device to the public Cook County open-data provider. That provider receives the request and ordinary network metadata and applies its own privacy and logging practices. We do not control the provider's retention policies. See Cook County's open-data portal.

Photos, exports and backups

When you select photos, the selected data is copied to app-local storage. Exported PDFs, CSVs and case backups leave the app only when you deliberately share or save them through iOS. The receiving service may store them. Device or cloud backups configured in your operating system are outside the app's direct control. A full case export can include property details, notes and original attached photo bytes; handle it as private information.

Notifications

Optional reminders are scheduled locally on the device after you confirm a deadline. The app does not maintain a remote push-token service. Calendar information must be verified directly with the relevant office.

Website support form

If you voluntarily submit a message on the Sherwood support page, the website hosting provider may process the information you enter so that we can receive and respond to the request. Avoid including sensitive case files, government ID numbers or private documents in a support message.

Updates and questions

We will update this policy if the released app's data behavior changes. See the PropertyTax Free support page for help and contact options.
