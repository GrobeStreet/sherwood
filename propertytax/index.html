A clear view of the facts

The app is being built around source records and your confirmed evidence. It will not invent property defects, predict a win or turn an assessment difference into promised tax savings.

Property

Know which record you are reading

Assessment components, building characteristics and source dates stay together. A missing field stays unknown; a different year or assessment stage is not silently substituted.

Comparison

Understand why a home is relevant

Review similar properties and their differences. Higher-assessed comparable homes remain visible, and you choose the records that support your explanation.

Evidence

Keep the original and a usable copy

Import documents and images, scan a notice, review on-device text recognition and assemble selected exhibits into an owner-reviewed PDF. Export a complete case backup separately.

First release

One county, with clear boundaries

Initial coverage is Cook County, Illinois. Automated comparisons are limited to supported detached-home cases with a single unambiguous parcel and building record.

Condominiums, commercial properties, multiple buildings or related PINs, and other complex cases require different analysis. Record access and evidence organization do not mean automated comparison is supported.

Read the coverage details

Your filing remains yours

Prepare here. Submit to the official office.

The app provides separate Assessor and Board of Review guidance. You open the official service, complete its required forms and submit there.

Deadline dates in the first build are entered and confirmed by you from an official notice or calendar. Local reminders use those saved dates; they are not an automatically refreshed county schedule.

How dates and filing work

Your documents stay under your control

Cases, original documents, text recognition and packet preparation are designed to stay on your device. Live record searches send the property query directly to the official data provider. Sharing a file is your choice.
