Development support guide

Help with your records and evidence

PropertyTax Free is in development and has not been released on the App Store. This guide describes the current build's intended workflow and limits.

Coverage

The first county is Cook County, Illinois. Automated assessment comparisons target straightforward detached homes in supported residential classes 202–209, with matching years and assessment stages and complete, unambiguous building and land records.

A record can be available even when comparison is unsupported. The app should explain the specific issue—such as multiple cards, related PINs, missing areas or special proration—while keeping evidence organization and official links available.

Common questions

Why does my assessment differ from my notice?

Check the PIN, tax year and assessment stage first. Mailed, Assessor-certified and Board-certified values can represent different points in the process. The data may also have changed after the app's saved snapshot.

Keep the notice and saved source. Getting a fresh record should create a separate case snapshot so the earlier evidence is preserved. A local edit never changes the county's record.

Why did the app find no suitable comparisons?

Missing data, unsupported property structure or a limited candidate pool can prevent a responsible comparison. The app should show these conditions, rather than fill the screen with unrelated homes. A lack of app-selected comparisons is not a decision about your right to file.

You can inspect a known comparable PIN, keep relevant documents and review the official office's requirements.

Does a lower assessment per square foot prove I am over-assessed?

No. The displayed building measure is building assessed value divided by building square footage. Land, property characteristics, assessment stage and relevant differences still matter. A difference is evidence to inspect; it is not an appraisal, a legal entitlement or a promised tax reduction.

What happens to a scanned or imported original?

The app saves original imported bytes separately from previews and recognized text, with a checksum to detect changes. The generated PDF may contain a readable derivative; the full case backup retains the original.

Text recognition is performed on-device and can make mistakes. Confirm the PIN, year, value and date against the original. A date found on a notice is not automatically an appeal deadline.

Why did an evidence PDF fail to export?

A selected original may be missing, changed, locked, restricted, damaged or in an unsupported format. Export stops rather than silently omitting it. Keep the original, import an authorized readable PDF/image/text copy, or explicitly exclude that item from the packet and handle it separately in the official process.

Current implementation limits are 32 MB per original, 64 MB of originals and 100 items per case, and 400 pages per PDF packet. Multi-frame images require a readable PDF copy for packet inclusion. Official filing services may have smaller upload limits; check them before filing.

How do I back up or move a case?

Use Export case backup in Evidence and save the .ptfbackup file through Files. It contains the case, source snapshots and every original evidence file, including items excluded from the PDF.

Use About & backups → Restore a case backup to restore it. A restored case gets a new identity; it does not overwrite an existing one. A PDF or CSV alone is not a complete case backup.

What does deleting a case remove?

It removes the app's local case and related originals and local exports. Copies you already shared, saved to a cloud destination or included in an operating-system backup remain under your control. The app cannot recall them.

Dates and official filing

The current build lets you save dates from your notice or an official calendar and label them as owner-entered. Complaint and evidence dates are separate. The app does not claim to download or continuously refresh the live county schedule.

Local notifications use the dates saved on your device. They do not guarantee delivery or learn about a county extension automatically. Recheck the official source before filing, especially the exact cutoff time. A date-only entry must not be read as permission to file until midnight.

  • Choose the correct office, township and tax year.
  • Complete the official complaint or application, not only the supporting packet.
  • Check its required documents, permitted representation and upload limits.
  • Submit through the official service and save its confirmation.

An exported packet is not a filing. A local “filed by me” status is your report; the app has not independently confirmed it with the county.

Contact support

Email bobby@trainingties.com with the app version, your iOS version and the steps that failed. A screenshot with personal details removed is often enough to explain an issue.

Please do not send original notices, financial documents, passwords or complete case backups unless you have deliberately chosen what to disclose. County staff, rather than app support, determine official records, filing acceptance and appeal decisions.

PropertyTax Free is an independent Sherwood Free tool. It is not a government service, law firm or appraisal service, and it does not guarantee a reduction. Consult the current official requirements for your case.
