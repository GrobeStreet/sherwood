Development privacy notice

Your case stays yours

This notice describes the current PropertyTax Free implementation. The app is not publicly released. The final shipped build and its App Store privacy answers must match its verified data flows.

Updated November 27, 2026 · Publisher: Sherwood Free

What stays on your device

The app stores saved property snapshots, selected comparisons, your explanations, original evidence files, document notes and saved reminder dates in its local app storage. Document scanning, text recognition and PDF preparation use on-device Apple frameworks.

The app does not require an app account and does not include advertising, tracking SDKs, a paid AI service or a developer-operated property lookup server. The current code does not send your case documents to Sherwood Free.

What uses a network connection

ActionWhere information goes
Search or refresh official recordsThe address or PIN, selected year and requested public-record fields go directly to Cook County's data service. The service also receives ordinary network information such as your IP address.
Open an official filing page or sourceYour browser connects to that website. Its account, cookie, log and privacy practices apply. Official portal credentials are handled there.
Open a Sherwood help pageThe website host receives an ordinary web request and associated network information. These pages add no advertising or analytics scripts.
Share or save an exportThe file goes to the destination you choose through the iOS share sheet or Files. Its provider's storage and privacy practices apply.
Email supportYour email address, message and any attachments you choose to send go to the support mailbox.

A public property record is still information about a property. We request only fields needed for the app's workflow. Normal lookup does not request owner names, mailing identities or buyer/seller identities.

Camera, imported files and reminders

Camera access is requested for document scanning when you choose that feature. You select imported files yourself. Notifications are optional and used for local reminders based on saved dates; denying them does not prevent evidence preparation.

Original images and PDFs can contain embedded metadata or personal information. Preview the selected exhibits before sharing. The app does not promise to remove every sensitive detail automatically.

Backups and sharing

A complete .ptfbackup file contains your case and all original attachments, including items excluded from the public evidence packet. Treat it as a private document. Portable backups are not encrypted by this app after export.

The operating system may include app data in device backups, depending on your settings. Saving an export to iCloud Drive or another cloud service is also your choice. The app does not provide an automatic account-based synchronization service.

Deleting data

You can delete a local case or remove an original evidence item from within the app. The app also cleans up temporary local exports. Previously shared files, email attachments and external or device backups are outside the app's control and must be managed at those destinations.

If you have emailed support, you can ask us to delete support messages we control by contacting bobby@trainingties.com. We cannot delete records maintained by a county or another independent service on your behalf.

Security and accuracy

Local case files use iOS file protection. Original evidence has a checksum so the app can detect changes before backup or packet export. This does not independently prove the contents, capture date or authenticity of a document.

Text recognition can be wrong. Its output is a suggestion for you to compare with the original, not a verified source value.

Questions and updates

Contact bobby@trainingties.com with questions about this notice. Changes to the implementation, integrations or support practices will require a corresponding notice update before release.
