Development methodology

Make the evidence understandable

Every comparison should be traceable to the record, period and calculation behind it. This page describes the approach being implemented and tested.

Official source records

The app queries Cook County's public data service directly. It preserves relevant source references, retrieval dates and available source-response snapshots with the saved case. A dataset's publisher update date and the time the app fetched a record are different facts.

  • Assessor — Assessed Values: land, building and total values by assessment year and publication stage.
  • Assessor — Residential Improvement Characteristics: building and land characteristics, including indicators that may make automated comparison unsuitable.
  • Assessor — Parcel Addresses: property address and PIN matching.
  • Assessor — Parcel Universe: township, neighborhood and parcel context.
  • Assessor — Parcel Sales: recorded transaction context and available qualifications.

How assessment candidates are compared

  1. Confirm the subject property and supported parcel/building structure.
  2. Use a consistent assessment year, stage, class and relevant neighborhood.
  3. Check building and land area and other reliable characteristics.
  4. Explain differences and uncertainty before presenting assessed-value measures.
  5. Let the homeowner inspect and select the records used in their explanation.

Similarity ordering is an aid to review. It is not a chance of winning. Similar properties with higher assessments are not hidden merely because they weaken a reduction argument.

Building assessed value per square foot is building assessed value divided by building square footage. Land and total assessment are shown separately. The Board's presentation guidance provides context for the relevant evidence.

What the comparison does not establish

A median, difference or ratio is not an appraisal, a legal entitlement or a predicted tax saving. The final bill involves more than a property's assessment. Missing values remain unknown; they are not turned into zero.

Sales records have separate cautions. A recording date is not necessarily a closing date. An absent flag does not prove an arm's-length sale. Multiparcel or related-party transactions may be unsuitable, and the app does not invent generic dollar adjustments for bedrooms, condition or size.

Deadlines and source freshness

The current app does not automatically refresh official calendars. Owners enter and confirm dates from notices or official publications, with complaint and evidence dates kept separate. Saved reminders do not establish that a filing window is still open.

Check the Assessor calendar or Board calendar before acting. An exact cutoff time is not inferred from a date-only publication.

Your statement and original evidence

The packet uses your explanation and selected documents. It does not generate defects, signatures, credentials or professional opinions. Recognized text must be checked against the original.

Imported originals are preserved, while packet copies may be scaled or laid out for readability. A complete backup includes the originals and saved source data. A source link or checksum does not independently verify the truth of a document.

Current validation boundary

Source connections, representative examples and comparison rules are being tested. The native iPhone build, complete PDF appearance, scanning, accessibility and official portal upload workflow still require release validation. This page does not certify that a finished app is live.
