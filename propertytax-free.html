Know your assessment. Keep your evidence. Pay nothing.

PropertyTax Free is a free iPhone research tool for homeowners who want to understand Cook County property assessments, examine similar properties, and organize evidence for an independent appeal. No subscription, paywall, ads or required app account.

Official records

Look up Cook County parcel information, assessment values and property characteristics, with source dates attached.

Explainable comparisons

Review suitable candidate property assessments and inspect why those properties are similar or different.

Your evidence folder

Keep notes, photos and local case records on your device, then export a research packet and comparison CSV.

DIY filing guidance

Reach the official Assessor and Board of Review rules and verify your filing window before acting.

Where we start

The initial automated comparison flow is limited to adequately documented Cook County single-family homes with one parcel and one building card. Other property types need manual review. Additional jurisdictions are not yet supported.

This independent tool is not affiliated with Cook County or any government office. It is not a law firm, valuation professional or automatic filing service. It cannot guarantee a lower assessment, tax savings, an appeal outcome or a current filing deadline. The app is still being tested and is not yet available for download.
