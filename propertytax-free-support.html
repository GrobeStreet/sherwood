PropertyTax Free — Support

This page supports the planned Cook County iPhone app, currently in development. The app is independently built and is not a county filing portal.

How the app works

Find a property by its 14-digit Cook County PIN or matching street address, check the returned property and assessment year, then review eligible comparison candidates. Select evidence, add homeowner notes and photos, and export the research packet. The owner must verify official forms and deadlines and file directly with the relevant government office.

Common questions

Why can't I compare my property?

Automated comparison requires complete year-matched records and a supported single-family one-card property. Other properties can require manual analysis.

Why did my search fail?

County public endpoints can be unavailable or slow. Retry using the exact 14-digit PIN. An outage is not proof that the county has no record.

Does the app file my appeal?

No. It helps prepare research and links to official filing resources. Your submission, receipt and outcome remain your responsibility.

Can my assessment increase?

Outcomes vary. Review the current official rules and the Board of Review's statements about changes to an incorrect assessment before filing.

Does a case export include photos?

The intended complete case backup includes the original locally attached photo bytes. Store exported files securely and verify restore before deleting device data.
